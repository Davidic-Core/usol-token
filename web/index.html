01 — Overview

An existing token, documented plainly.

UnitedSOLANA (USOL) is a Classic SPL Token deployed on Solana Mainnet. The project's recorded metadata description characterizes it as a community and utility token; that description is not an independent verification of utility.

This is the project's official website. It publishes information that can be sourced from the project's pinned Mainnet record, and it keeps recorded facts separate from live or third-party data.

This site presents recorded facts separately from live or third-party information and avoids claims that cannot be established from the available project records.

What this site does not do

  • It does not sell, swap, or transfer tokens.
  • It does not ask for a seed phrase or private key.
  • It does not publish prices, market data, or return projections.
  • It does not require a wallet connection to read.

03 — Principles

Three standards for everything we publish.

01

Speed with clarity

The site is built to load quickly and read clearly on a phone, with no trackers or wallet dependencies, and no scripts needed to read any page.

02

One unified record

Official information comes from a single pinned Mainnet record. The same fact is not restated differently in different places.

03

Transparency by default

Recorded facts, live data, and third-party information are labelled separately. Nothing is described as verified when it is not.

04 — Transparency

What we publish, and how we label it.

Recorded facts

Deployment details come from the project's pinned Mainnet record. They are described as recorded at deployment, not as a fresh live check.

Verified sources

Links and assets are published only after their destination or provenance is confirmed. No unofficial community links appear on this site.

No invented metrics

We do not publish prices, market capitalisation, trading volume, liquidity, listings, partnerships, audits, or guaranteed outcomes.

Labelled live data

Any future market or on-chain data will name its source and last-checked time, and will show a clear failure state rather than stale values.

05 — Risk

  • Check before you act. This site does not offer trading, swaps, staking, or returns, and does not require a wallet connection.
